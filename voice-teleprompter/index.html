How it works

It listens for speech, not words.

Voice mode detects when someone is speaking. The script moves at the chosen speed while speech is present, waits during pauses, and resumes when speech returns.

Any language or accent

There is no transcript to match. Voice activity works the same way whether the script is in English, Spanish, Portuguese, or another language.

Adjustable sensitivity

A live mic meter and sensitivity control help tune Voice mode for a quiet room, a distant microphone, or a noisier set.

Private by design

Microphone input is processed on the device to detect speech. Voice Teleprompter does not record or transmit the audio.

Lite for the essentials. PRO for the full setup.

Privacy and reliability

Your script remains yours.

Voice Teleprompter has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or separate account system.

  • Voice processing stays on device. Microphone audio is immediately discarded after speech activity is detected.
  • Reading works offline. Saved scripts and the teleprompter remain available without a network connection.
  • Lite stores scripts locally. Nothing leaves the device unless the user chooses Share.
  • PRO uses private iCloud storage. Automatic sync connects directly to Apple and keeps scripts available offline.

Questions

What to know before downloading.

Does Voice Teleprompter recognize specific words?

No. It detects speech activity instead of transcribing or matching words. The script moves while you speak and waits when you pause.

Does voice scrolling work in any language?

Yes. Because the app listens for speech rather than vocabulary, Voice mode works with any language and accent.

Can I import a Word document or PDF?

PRO imports DOCX, PDF, RTF, and plain-text files from Files, iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, Mail, and other installed file providers. Document import is not included in Lite.

Does the app record video?

No. Voice Teleprompter is deliberately focused on prompting and script reading. It does not access the camera or record video.

Can I move scripts from Lite to PRO?

Yes, manually. Touch and hold a saved script in Lite, choose Share, and save a text or RTF copy. Import that file into PRO. Installing PRO does not automatically move Lite scripts.

How does PRO sync scripts?

PRO syncs through the private CloudKit database attached to the user’s iCloud account. Scripts remain available offline, and Pudu Digital does not receive an Apple ID or a server-side copy.

Is there a subscription?

No. Lite is free. PRO is a separate one-time App Store purchase with no monthly subscription and no separate app account.
